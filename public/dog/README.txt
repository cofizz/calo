🐶 Sister's dog reaction photos
================================

Drop photos in THIS folder (c:\Users\Filip\Desktop\CALAPP\public\dog\).
You can add 3-4 (or more!) photos per mood — one is picked at RANDOM each time.

Name each photo "<mood>-<anything>.<ext>", for example:
  under-goal-1.jpg, under-goal-2.jpg, under-goal-3.png
  near-goal-1.jpg, near-goal-2.jpg ...
  hit-goal-1.jpg, hit-goal-buddy.png ...
  over-limit-1.jpg, over-limit-2.jpg ...
  way-over-1.jpg, way-over-2.jpg ...

The 5 photo buckets (by daily calories vs your goal):
  under-goal-*   -> under 50% of goal
  near-goal-*    -> 50%-90% of goal
  hit-goal-*     -> around your goal (90%-110%)
  over-limit-*   -> over goal (110%-150%)
  way-over-*     -> way over goal (above 150%)

NOTE: the photo is chosen by calorie level, but the MESSAGE the dog gives
adapts to your plan (cut / bulk / maintain). E.g. eating under your goal gets
a "good boy, nice deficit" on a CUT, but an "eat more!" on a BULK.

Allowed file types: .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .gif
Tips:
- Square-ish photos look best (shown in a small rounded box).
- If a mood has no photos yet, a friendly emoji shows instead — nothing breaks.
- After adding/replacing photos, just refresh the app in the browser.
